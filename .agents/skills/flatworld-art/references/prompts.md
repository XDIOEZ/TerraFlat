```text
Style: low-saturation chunky retro pixel art; natural proportions, large square pixel blocks, broad connected color masses, hard stepped edges, few large shadow/highlight shapes.
Task: <主体、动作、用途>.
References: <逐图注明身份、结构、画风或姿势职责>.
Pixel density: match the approved current bear/player grain at the intended in-game size; outline and every feature share one coarse logical pixel grid. <世界显示尺度或 UI 槽位大小>.
Layout: <方向、显示尺度、画布/帧网格、Pivot/基线、留白>.
Background: <独立轮廓透明；满格连片块不透明>.
Keep/change: <锁定身份、配色、光源、像素尺度；只改指定部分>.
Exclude: high-definition pixel art, dense fur/wood-grain/scratch strokes, tiny scattered highlights, fine gradients, high-resolution illustration with a pixelation filter; <本次需排除的背景、投影、文字或多余主体>.
```

动物朝向、设备连接层和立绘构图从各自参考补充；用户指定其他画风时替换 Style。
