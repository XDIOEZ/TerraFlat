unity-voronoi-water - flow animation adaptation
Source: https://github.com/cbunt/unity-voronoi-water
Revision: de93169d97ec25f4b2e72eff5834f2360ffe4b99
Original: Assets/Voronoi Water Shader/Voronoi Water.shadergraph
License: MIT No Attribution (MIT-0)

FlatWorld adapts the world-space half-cycle flow sampling and triangular crossfade.
The original 4D Voronoi, ShaderGraph, meshes and art are not distributed.
The sampling noise, 2D lighting, water depth, weather and current inputs are FlatWorld code.
Adaptation: Assets/9_Shaders/Shader/WaterSurfaceFlow.hlsl
