# Space sound effects

Sci-fi Sounds 1.0 by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds and https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-sounds), licensed under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Used for spaceship door opening/closing, ignition and landing. Original OGG recordings are imported as mono 48 kHz PCM with runtime volume adjustments. Attribution is optional under the original release; thank you, Kenney.

Campfire.wav by Defaultv (https://freesound.org/people/Defaultv/sounds/536935/), licensed under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Existing processed campfire recording is reused at a lower playback pitch for the spaceship propulsion loop.
