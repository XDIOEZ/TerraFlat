# Combat SFX credits

- Impact Sounds and RPG Audio — Kenney — https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds and https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio — CC0 1.0 — combat material impacts and blocked hits. Selected individual recordings converted to mono 48 kHz PCM, trimmed, faded and level adjusted.
- Medieval sound effects - Weapon Textures — Ben Jaszczak and Brian Nelson / Still North Media — https://opengameart.org/node/146862 — CC0 1.0 — melee movement, bow, crossbow and sling-release Foley. Three independent takes cut from each source recording; no synthesized variants. Pickaxe uses katana air-swish Foley; sling uses seax air-swish Foley.
- Blow on piece #0748 — Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank — https://bigsoundbank.com/blow-on-piece-s0748.html — CC0 1.0 — blowpipe-release air Foley. Three separate bursts trimmed and level adjusted.

Ice uses brittle glass Foley; foliage uses cloth Foley. All files are adapted recordings, not merely references. Existing tree-chopping credits remain in Tree-Chopping-SFX-CREDITS.md. Per-clip source offsets and hashes: SoundDesign/Combat_Impacts_CC0/sources.json.
