FlatWorld realistic 2D cloud-layer algorithm references

jbritain/glimmer-shaders
Source: https://github.com/jbritain/glimmer-shaders
Revision: 6a591097b6348155a4ba5a1aab92b79a2c9896d3
Studied: shaders/lib/atmosphere/clouds.glsl
Reference: continuous noise cloud density, coordinate warping, plane-layer
optical thickness, exponential transmittance, and light-direction density.
FlatWorld independently adapts these ideas to a URP 2D world overlay:
offline periodic Perlin/Worley fields, four runtime data-texture samples,
two light-direction samples, and its own wind integration and player mask.
The data image is generated locally by Tools/Rendering/generate_cloud_density.py.

tuxalin/procedural-tileable-shaders
Source: https://github.com/tuxalin/procedural-tileable-shaders
Revision: 3b867954908418427683f705671b236db0454235
Studied: fbm.glsl, warp.glsl
Reference: periodic multi-octave noise and domain warping.
FlatWorld's generator uses NumPy gradient noise and periodic feature points.
No upstream image assets are used.
